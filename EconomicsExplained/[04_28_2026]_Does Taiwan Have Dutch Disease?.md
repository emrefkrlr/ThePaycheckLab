* **Main Topic:** The potential economic risk of "Dutch Disease" in *Taiwan* due to its overwhelming reliance on the semiconductor industry.
* **Target Audience:** Geopolitically minded individuals, economics students, investors, and general audiences interested in global technology supply chains.
* **Key Themes:** 1) The "Silicon Shield" and semiconductor dominance; 2) The mechanism of Dutch Disease; 3) Economic inequality and a "two-speed" economy; 4) Geopolitical and geological risk factors; 5) Strategies for economic diversification and currency stabilization.
