## 1. Core Concept & Metadata
* **Main Topic:** The inadequacy of current economic metrics in measuring the real-world impact of AI on the labor market, specifically highlighted by the MIT *Iceberg Index*.
* **Target Audience:** Professionals, investors, policymakers, and general viewers interested in the intersection of technology, labor economics, and the future of work.
* **Key Themes:** 
    1. The fallacy of tracking "jobs" instead of "tasks."
    2. The "visible" vs. "underwater" economic exposure to AI.
    3. Misalignment of government/corporate workforce planning tools.
    4. The "Cost Disease" (Baumol's effect) as a consequence of AI-driven productivity.
    5. The transition to a two-speed economy.

---

## 2. The Hook & Opening (First 30 Seconds)
* **Hook Strategy:** Leveraging existing anxieties regarding AI-induced layoffs (e.g., programmers/junior developers) and then immediately subverting the viewer's expectations by labeling the mainstream discourse as "focused on the wrong thing."
* **Core Promise:** To provide a more accurate, data-driven map of how AI will actually reshape the economy, moving beyond the sensationalist headlines.

---

## 3. Data Presentation & Credibility
* **Information Delivery:** The narrator uses analogies (e.g., "navigating a new city using a 30-year-old map") to explain abstract economic concepts. Quantitative data is framed as a "risk map" to make technical findings accessible.
* **Authority Building:** The video relies heavily on the *MIT Iceberg Index* report, *ONET* (Department of Labor database), and research from *Anthropic*, framing these as objective, high-level sources that surpass standard news reporting.

---

## 4. Structure & Content Flow
* **Chapter Breakdown:**
    * **Introduction:** Defining the disconnect between AI anxiety and reality (0:00-1:30).
    * **The Iceberg Index Methodology:** Explaining how MIT maps skills vs. jobs (1:30-7:26).
    * **Findings (The Under-surface Data):** Analyzing why white-collar administrative/finance roles are more exposed than tech jobs (7:26-12:15).
    * **Failure of Current Metrics:** Why governments are looking at the wrong numbers (12:15-12:53).
    * **Baumol’s Cost Disease:** Why non-AI-exposed roles (trades, healthcare) become more expensive (12:53-15:47).
    * **Conclusion:** The risk of a two-speed economy (15:47-16:52).
* **Pacing & Retention:** The video maintains high engagement by alternating between macro-economic theory and concrete, real-world examples (e.g., the string quartet vs. the software engineer).
