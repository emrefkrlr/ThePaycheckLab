# Scene 1 - Introduction to the Lab

```
Style: Vox-style paper collage animation, stop-motion aesthetic, layered
hand-cut paper cutouts with visible torn and cut edges, subtle paper grain
and fiber texture, soft drop shadows between layers creating gentle depth,
matte paper surface with no glossy reflections.

Color palette: soft pastel tones throughout, with #B31942 (muted pastel red)
and #0A3161 (muted pastel navy) as recurring accent colors against a warm
off-white background. Colors desaturated and airy, never neon or saturated.

Lighting: soft, even, diffused studio light from top-left, minimal harsh
shadows, gentle ambient occlusion between paper layers.

Camera: slow subtle parallax movement between paper layers (2.5D effect),
no fast pans, no shaky motion, calm and deliberate framing.

Motion: gentle stop-motion style movement, slight paper flutter, elements
sliding/rotating into frame like physical paper being placed by hand,
subtle jitter typical of stop-motion, not smooth CGI motion.

Format: 16:9 widescreen, cinematic composition.

Audio instructions: Cinematic background music, ambient sound design,
mechanical foley, and paper rustling sound effects ONLY. Strictly NO
voiceover, NO narration, NO speaking.

---

Scene content: Opens on a paper-cutout desert cityscape silhouette of
Phoenix, Arizona at dusk — layered paper mountains, tall paper cutout
office towers with pastel navy windows, a stylized paper sun glowing
pastel red-orange low on the horizon. In the foreground, a hand-cut paper
calendar/notepad page flutters into frame with a faint paper question
mark and a small paper coin stack sitting on it, as if the scene itself
is asking a budgeting question. The paper coin stack gently wobbles and
resettles. Midway through, the camera parallax-shifts to reveal a paper
cutout fast food counter silhouette at the edge of frame — a simple
paper apron and a paper name-tag shape, hinting at a worker without
showing a full character yet. A single paper cutout of a folded $15
bill/wage tag drifts down and lands softly beside the counter, settling
with a soft paper rustle as the scene closes.

Camera/motion note: Begin wide on the skyline, slow parallax push-in
toward the fast food counter silhouette, ending on the wage tag settling
in the final 2 seconds.
```
